# E6 · Coherence Across Scales

**Preregistered finite synthetic comparison. Nothing in E6 measures human consciousness, coherence of a biological system, physical crystals, or a fundamental golden-ratio law.**

## Question

For six independently generated, normalized hypothetical scale measurements, does a fixed `φ` weighting predict a synthesized target more accurately than **equal weights** or alternative weighting schemes under matched computational cost?

Crucially the target is generated by a **known programmed profile**. We intentionally create worlds matched to each candidate, so a profile's success is a sanity check, **not** independent physical evidence. Different engineered regimes favor different profiles.

## Frozen protocol

- Six scale inputs `x[0..5]`, each in `[0,1]`, deterministically generated by unsigned xorshift32.
- Five programmed ground-truth regimes: `equal`, `phi_inner`, `phi_outer`, `center`, `alternating`.
- Exactly the same five candidate profiles.
- Weight vectors normalized to sum to 1:
  - `equal`: (1,1,1,1,1,1)
  - `phi_inner`: (φ⁵,φ⁴,φ³,φ²,φ,1)
  - `phi_outer`: (1,φ,φ²,φ³,φ⁴,φ⁵)
  - `center`: (1,2,3,3,2,1)
  - `alternating`: (4,1,4,1,4,1).
- Synthetic target `y=Σ w_world[i] × x[i] + ε`, with bounded, deterministic jitter `ε ∈ [-0.02,0.02]`.
- Fixed validation seed **202**, test seed **203**, **128 samples per regime per split**.
- Choose the lowest *validation* mean absolute error from the five candidates with lexicographic tie-break, then freeze it. Report errors for **all candidates** on held-out test, without retuning.
- Report MAE and RMSE; every profile uses precisely **six readings and six weighted terms**.
- Negative control: shift test labels by 47 positions without changing predictions or re-fitting.
- Inspect the five regimes separately, including a world where equal weights have the correct ground truth.
- No data-dependent choice of φ or the generator after observing test outcomes.

## What is and is not a success

- If the equal-world favors equal weights, the benchmark correctly refuses φ universal optimality.
- If a φ-ground-truth world favors the matching φ profile, this merely validates the implementation against a designed fixture.
- A validation-selected candidate may correctly adapt to a known family but this is **not** evidence of universal intelligence improvement.
- The reversed φ profile tests orientation dependence.
- The shuffled-label error tests whether feature-target pairing matters; it is not a p-value or independent physical falsification.

**Promotion requires** independently measured phenomena, prospective holdouts, matched alternatives, effect uncertainty, interventions where possible, and replicated studies. No φ/biological advantage is claimed by E6.
